Final Proposal

For my final project, I want to make something similar to r/place, a social experiment and collaborative art project launched in 2017 where users placed pixels on a massive canvas, but were forced to collaborate on art by only being able to place one pixel every 5 minutes.

I want to make a similar page where users can make art and see other users' art, but without having to actively collaborate. My concept is a large fridge where anyone can draw something and put it on the fridge, but users can then interact with the drawings and add on to them. I want to make this project as I think it will be a fun to see what people will draw when given complete freedom and will be an interesting challenge to implement technically.
